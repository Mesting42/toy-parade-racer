# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: Vite, Three.js and modular JavaScript for an offline capable browser game.

## Users

Single players on desktop, tablet, and phone who want short, approachable kart races against five computer drivers.

## Product Purpose

《玩具巡游赛》 is a complete arcade kart racer set on hand-built tabletop circuits. Players learn to steer, drift, choose a racing line, and time temporary items through quick races, three cups, time trials, and pressure-free practice.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Six racers start each race; cup races contain three laps. Nine distinct circuits form three cups. Cars have different acceleration, top speed, handling, drift charge, and item resistance. Six temporary item types, coins, unlockable cosmetics, a best-time ghost, three AI difficulties, keyboard, gamepad, multitouch, pause/resume, settings, and local progress are supported. The first-run control lesson is skippable. Runs locally without remote assets or services. Save damage or unavailable storage must not block play; destructive progress reset requires an explicit confirmation.

## Brand Commitments

The game is Chinese-first and uses an original, bright, warm, hand-built toy world: painted wood, cardboard, soft clay, fabric, sunlight, model karts, and tabletop set pieces. It must remain clearly distinct from 《夜行赛道》 and avoid realistic street racing, night-city neon, pixel art, and simulated-car dashboards.

## Product Principles

- Keep the racing line and the next hazard readable.
- Reward active steering and a timed drift release.
- Keep item effects brief, visible, and counterable.
- Let every mode start and finish through a clear playable flow.
- Make all content available through play without paid advantages.
