Usage: Six complete driverless toy vehicles (vehicle-racer-low, vehicle-racer, vehicle-speedster, vehicle-drag-racer, vehicle-vintage-racer, vehicle-suv), finish arch, modular track shoulders, three themed ramp variants and model trees. The player chooses one of the original three racers; the five rivals use the remaining five distinct vehicle models. Runtime assembly adds body paint, enamel rear numbers, glossy lacquer and wider tires. Vehicle length is 6.15 world units; source -Z is rotated to game +Z. The three additional GLBs were copied from the retained original archive on 2026-10-19.
No runtime external download or CDN is required.
